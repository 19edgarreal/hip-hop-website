Hip Hop Around the World

Hip hop expanded from the United States to countries around the world and influenced many different cultures.

Artists in different countries began using hip hop to express their experiences, ideas, and creativity.

Important Moments

  1. Hip hop develops in the Bronx.
  2. Hip hop records become popular.
  3. Hip hop expands internationally.
  4. Hip hop becomes a global culture.
