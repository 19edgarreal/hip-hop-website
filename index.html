The Origins of Hip Hop

Hip hop began in the Bronx, New York, during the 1970s. It developed as a cultural movement that included music, dance, art, and fashion.

DJs, MCs, dancers, and artists helped shape hip hop into a movement that gave communities a way to express themselves creatively.

The Four Elements of Hip Hop

  • DJing: creating music and beats.
  • MCing: rapping and performing lyrics.
  • Breakdancing: expressing music through dance.
  • Graffiti: creating visual art.
