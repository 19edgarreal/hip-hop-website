Learn about the origins and evolution of hip hop.

The Origins of Hip Hop

Hip hop began in the Bronx, New York, during the 1970s. It developed as a cultural movement that included music, dance, art, and fashion.

Early hip hop culture was influenced by DJs, MCs, breakdancers, and graffiti artists. These elements helped create the culture that became known around the world.

Four Elements of Hip Hop

  • DJing
  • MCing
  • Breakdancing
  • Graffiti

Hip Hop Through the Years

During the 1980s and 1990s, hip hop continued to grow and became an important part of popular culture.

Today, hip hop is a global genre and cultural movement that continues to influence music, fashion, art, and entertainment.
